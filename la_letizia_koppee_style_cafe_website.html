Our Story

Located on the 5th Floor opposite Quest Mall in Park Circus, La Letizia is Kolkata's premier specialty coffee house and late-night lounge.

Whether you're dropping by for an afternoon artisanal espresso, catching up with friends over gourmet platters, or soaking in our vibrant late-night ambiance until 5:00 AM, every cup and plate is crafted to perfection. Rated 4.5★ by over 1,800 delighted guests across Kolkata.
